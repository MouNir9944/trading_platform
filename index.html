Every pair in the rail is scanned with the same seven gates; the bars show how many opened. Click one to load it. On the chart, the plan is drawn where you'd actually place it: entry line, stop below or above it, target at the nearest swing, and the two shaded zones are what you risk against what you're playing for. The volume profile on the right is built from the visible candles — the amber line is the point of control, the brighter bars are the 70% value area. VWAP resets each UTC day; HH / HL / LH / LL mark swing structure. Below 15m the gates flip fast and spreads and fees eat a bigger share of every R — treat 1m as a timing tool for an entry you already decided on from a higher timeframe, not as the frame you make the decision on. Thresholds (1.5 ATR stop, RSI 45–72, 2R minimum, 75% node block) live in the analyse() function — change them to your own. Public market data only, no API key, nothing stored.
